Biological Fundamentals

The 4 Phases of Microbial Batch Culture

How our IoT sensors and Python analytics translate physical biology into predictive computational insights.

Phase 1

Lag Phase (Adaptation)

Upon inoculation, microorganisms do not divide immediately. Cells are actively synthesizing essential enzymes, ribosomes, and adapting to the nutrient medium.

Sensor Signal: Turbidity flat (~100 NTU) CO₂: Baseline ~420 PPM
Phase 2

Log Phase (Exponential Growth)

Cells multiply at maximum velocity through binary fission ($\mu_{max}$). Carbohydrate consumption produces vigorous respiration off-gassing and organic acids.

Sensor Signal: Turbidity surges rapidly pH drops; CO₂ spikes >2500 PPM
Phase 3

Stationary Phase (Peak Biomass)

Nutrients in the broth become exhausted while inhibitory byproducts accumulate. Net division rate slows to zero ($\frac{dN}{dt} \approx 0$).

Sensor Signal: Turbidity plateau (~790 NTU) ⚠️ HARVEST WINDOW ALERT
Phase 4

Death / Decline Phase

Without nutrient supplementation, autolysis and toxic byproduct buildup cause viable cells to die faster than new ones form. Harvesting late degrades quality.

Sensor Signal: Turbidity starts declining Respiration off-gas ceases

Mathematical Growth Modeling

Logistic Sigmoid
Primary Growth Equation:
N(t) = N₀ + (K - N₀) / (1 + e^(-r(t - t₀)))
Where K = Carrying capacity (peak biomass), r = Growth rate, t₀ = Midpoint inflection.
Harvest Decision Derivative:
dN/dt = ΔTurbidity / Δt → 0.0 NTU/hr
When the slope approaches zero after exponential acceleration, the system instantly notifies the operator: "Peak biomass reached. Collect sample now."

Embedded IoT Architecture

Hardware & Sensor Stack

Every physical parameter corresponds to a dedicated submerged or headspace sensor wired to the ESP32.

🟢

Optical Turbidity Sensor

Pin: GPIO 34 (ADC1)

Measures scattered light transmittance (NTU/OD). As cells multiply, broth cloudiness increases, serving as our primary biomass density proxy.

🌡️

DS18B20 Temp Probe

Pin: GPIO 4 (OneWire)

Waterproof digital temperature sensor with 4.7kΩ pull-up resistor. Tracks incubator stability and thermal spikes from exothermic cellular respiration.

🧪

Analog pH Electrode

Pin: GPIO 35 (ADC1)

Glass bulb electrode detecting hydronium concentration. Fermentative production of organic acids drops pH from ~7.1 to ~5.4, indicating vitality.

💨

MQ-135 Gas Sensor

Pin: GPIO 32 (ADC1)

Headspace gas sensor tracking carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) and volatile off-gases in PPM. Independent metabolic confirmation of respiration plateau.
