System Architecture

End-to-End Bioprocess Data Pipeline

01 / EDGE

ESP32 Edge Microcontroller

Deterministic acquisition firmware polling 12-bit ADC1 channels and OneWire thermal probes every 5–10 s, posting TLS-encrypted JSON telemetry packets directly to REST endpoints.

02 / STORAGE

Supabase PostgreSQL Schema

Relational time-series architecture isolating batch metadata (bioprocess_batches) from high-frequency sensor logs (bioprocess_readings) via indexed UUID foreign keys.

03 / ANALYTICS

SciPy Logistic Growth Engine

Applies moving-average signal denoising, performs non-linear least-squares regression on N(t), and computes numerical derivative dN/dt to detect stationary biomass saturation.

04 / CONTROL

Harvest Adherence & Audit

Logs exact UTC/IST inoculation timestamps, fires automated harvest window advisories at inflection plateau, and computes post-peak termination variance in minutes.

05 / ARCHIVE

Historical Batch Replay

One-click reconstruction of completed fermentations across 1m–1h timeline zoom intervals for yield comparison, QA verification, and laboratory reporting.

Bioprocess Kinetics

Four Phases of Microbial Batch Culture

Translating continuous optical density, broth acidification, and gas evolution signals into quantitative phase classification.

Phase 01

Lag Phase (Metabolic Adaptation)

dN/dt ≈ 0

Following inoculation, cells do not divide immediately. Metabolic energy is directed toward enzyme synthesis, RNA transcription, and environmental equilibrium.

Turbidity: ~105 NTU baseline CO2: ~420 PPM ambient
Phase 02

Log Phase (Exponential Velocity)

μ = μmax

Balanced binary fission proceeds at maximum specific growth rate (μmax). Vigorous substrate consumption produces rapid organic acid secretion and CO2 off-gassing.

Turbidity: Exponential surge pH drops; CO2 > 2,500 PPM
Phase 03

Stationary Phase (Carrying Capacity)

dN/dt → 0

Limiting carbon substrates approach exhaustion while metabolites accumulate. Cell division equals cell mortality, stabilizing turbidity near peak carrying capacity K.

Turbidity: Plateau (~790 NTU) OPTIMAL HARVEST WINDOW
Phase 04

Decline / Death Phase (Autolysis)

dN/dt < 0

Nutrient depletion and toxic accumulation trigger irreversible cell lysis. Optical density drops and cellular respiration diminishes; late harvesting degrades yield purity.

Turbidity: Declining slope Respiration ceases

Instrumentation Stack

Sensor Hardware & ESP32 Pinout

Four independent submerged and headspace sensing channels wired to high-resolution ADC1 and digital OneWire pins.

GPIO 34 · ADC1

Optical Turbidity Probe

Infrared phototransistor measuring suspended biomass light attenuation in Nephelometric Turbidity Units (NTU). Primary proxy for cell count N(t).

GPIO 04 · OneWire

DS18B20 Thermal Sensor

Submersible digital probe (4.7 kΩ pull-up) tracking incubator thermal drift and exothermic heat output during peak logarithmic fermentation.

GPIO 35 · ADC1

Analog Glass pH Electrode

Monitors hydronium ion concentration (0–14 pH). Acidification shifts from 7.1 toward 5.4 validate metabolic organic acid synthesis.

GPIO 32 · ADC1

MQ-135 Headspace Gas

Measures reactor headspace CO2 and volatile respiration gases in PPM, providing orthogonal confirmation of growth saturation.
